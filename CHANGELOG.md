## [1.0.0] — 2026-06-28

### Added
- 9 AI models with isolated webviews: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi, Le Mistral, HuggingChat
- Left sidebar with navigation, AI list with drag-and-drop reorder, pinned bookmarks, and footer controls
- Right sidebar with 9 floating productivity tools and vault quick-access popovers
- Vault with 6 tabs: Prompts, Responses, Codebook, Notebook, Screenshots, Whiteboards
- Command Palette (`Ctrl+K`) for searching AIs, actions, and shortcuts
- Bookmarks with auto-fetched chat names, 8 pinned slots, and 5 sidebar quick access slots
- Download manager with file type tabs, built-in image/video/audio viewer, and bulk operations
- Split view for side-by-side AI comparison (`Ctrl+\`)
- Content capture (`Ctrl+Shift+S`) and workspace capture (`Ctrl+Shift+X`)
- History view with time-grouped entries and AI filters
- Full keyboard shortcut support with searchable reference overlay (`Ctrl+/`)
- 5-step onboarding flow: Welcome, Name, Your AIs, Your Workspace, Finishing
- System tray with minimize-to-tray and launch-at-startup options
- Per-AI zoom persistence (50%–200%)
- Settings with custom data location and display name
- AI model toggle — disable unused AIs to save memory
- Home view with personalized greeting, quick actions, continue section, and pinned bookmarks
- Bug report view (`Ctrl+Shift+I`)
- Confirmation dialogs for destructive actions
- i18n support with English translations

### Tools
- Notes — Rich text editor with checklists, auto-save, and pin support
- Todo — Today tasks and long-term tasks with priority levels
- Time — World clock, stopwatch, and countdown timer with focus presets
- Calendar — Month view with events, holidays, and agenda
- Calculator — Standard and scientific modes with history
- Converter — Length, weight, temperature, volume, currency (live rates), and speed
- Whiteboard — Drawing canvas with 8 tools, zoom, undo/redo, and save to Vault
- Pomodoro — Focus sessions with breaks, statistics, and summary notes
- Scratchpad — Quick temporary notepad with save to Vault
