# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Thred free?
Yes. Thred is completely free and open source. The codebase is available on GitHub.

## Do I need an account?
No. Thred is local-first — no backend, no cloud service, no Thred account. You log into each AI service directly within the app.

## Is my data safe?
Yes. All your data — prompts, notes, bookmarks, screenshots, and settings — is stored locally on your machine. Each AI runs in an isolated, sandboxed webview. Thred never collects or transmits your personal data.

## Which operating systems are supported?
Windows 10/11 (x64).

## How do I switch between AI models?
Press `Alt+1` through `Alt+9`, or click the AI icon in the left sidebar.

## How do I save prompts and responses?
Select text in any AI chat and press `Ctrl+S` to save to Vault. Or open Vault (`Ctrl+L`) and click "New" to create items manually.

## How do I capture screenshots?
`Ctrl+Shift+S` captures the active AI chat. `Ctrl+Shift+X` captures the full workspace. Screenshots are saved to Vault.

## Where is my data stored?
In a folder you choose during setup. You can view or change the location in Settings > System > Data Location.

## How do I report a bug?
Press `Ctrl+Shift+I`, or go to Settings > About > Report Bug. Describe the issue and submit.

## Can I reorder the AI models?
Yes. Drag and drop AI icons in the sidebar, or hover and use the up/down arrow buttons.
